SANCHORE BAZAAR - UPDATE NOTES

1. Customer login ke baad Home screen par Logout button ab sahi kaam karega.
   - Firebase se sign out karega.
   - Login screen kholega.
   - Purani customer screens ko navigation history se hata dega.

2. Checkout mein Cash on Delivery aur Online Payment alag choices hain.
   Online Payment par secure Paytm checkout khulta hai. Paytm merchant account par
   jo methods enabled hain (UPI Apps, Cards, Net Banking etc.), wahi gateway page
   par customer ko milenge. App khud se Google Pay/PhonePe ko direct gateway nahi
   bana sakti; gateway account ki configuration zaroori hai.

3. Important: Paytm live merchant credentials aur activation ke bina real online
   payment chalne ka daava nahi kiya gaya hai. Flutter APK build is environment mein
   run nahi hua; FlutLab mein pub get aur build zaroor karein.
